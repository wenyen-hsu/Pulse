# Classic large-list failure accepted for the Liquid scatter trial

**Historical, not newly verified.** Current limitation: [../ui/panel-geometry.md](../ui/panel-geometry.md). Trial evidence and boundaries: [../../Downloads/README.md](../../Downloads/README.md).

## What happened

During native acceptance of the `f5370ee` Liquid trial on an Apple Silicon VM, the legacy Classic rail was not visible with 77 enabled accounts. Its count-sized window measured 6,838 points tall on a 1,200-point-high display. This was a Classic failure, not a Liquid packing or circular paging failure.

Nine-account Classic visibility and real-pointer interaction were separately verified. That result did not establish 77-account Classic usability. The accounts were labelled local positive-quota fixtures, not 77 live provider integrations.

## Why the trial kept the limitation

The user chose to disclose the Classic failure as a known limitation and complete Liquid trial verification and packaging instead of repairing the legacy layout in this task. Large lists could use Liquid pet or circular Pet orb. The Classic-77 failure was neither fixed nor counted as passed.

The accepted app was packaged without rebuilding or re-signing. This decision records the trial's scope and measurements; it is not evidence that later builds or other displays reproduce them.
